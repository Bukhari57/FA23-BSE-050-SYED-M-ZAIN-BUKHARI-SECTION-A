# POS Application Features

## 1. Customer Management

*   **Walk-in Customers:**
    *   Allow sales to be made without assigning a customer.
    *   Generate a generic receipt.
*   **Regular Customers:**
    *   Create and manage customer profiles with details like name, phone number, email, and address.
    *   Search for customers by name or phone number.
    *   View customer purchase history.

## 2. Ledger System

*   **Debit/Credit:**
    *   Maintain a ledger for each regular customer.
    *   Record all transactions, including sales (debit) and payments (credit).
*   **Payments:**
    *   Accept full or partial payments from customers.
    *   Record payment method (cash, card, etc.).
*   **Outstanding Balance:**
    *   Calculate and display the outstanding balance for each customer.
    *   Generate reports for accounts receivable.

## 3. Product Management

*   Add, edit, and delete products.
*   Organize products into categories.
*   Manage stock levels.
*   Set product prices and apply discounts.

## 4. Sales & Billing

*   Create new sales orders.
*   Add products to the cart.
*   Calculate taxes and totals.
*   Generate and print receipts.

## 5. Reporting & Analytics

*   View daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports.
*   Track top-selling products.
*   Monitor revenue and profit.
